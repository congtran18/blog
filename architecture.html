CHAPTER 02

Data Architecture & The SAP S/4HANA SSOT Boundary

A frequent mistake when designing enterprise FinTech platforms is trying to replicate the core ERP database inside microservices. In our design, SAP S/4HANA is the sole Single Source of Truth (SSOT) for financial master data and general ledger balances. Our MongoDB Atlas cluster functions purely as an operational transaction ledger and high-speed cache.

🍃 Why Only 2 Document Collections in MongoDB Atlas?

Instead of normalizing into dozens of relational tables, we deliberately consolidated our persistence model into 2 core Aggregate Roots:

  1. purchase_orders (Intent & Cache Aggregate): Caches customer order details, line-item container numbers, and calculated demurrage fees fetched from SAP. Mutable during checkout; read-only once submitted.
  2. payment_transactions (Financial Ledger Aggregate): Stores atomic payment attempts. An order can have multiple transaction attempts (e.g., customer cancels QR, bank drops connection), but exactly ONE successful transaction transitions to payStatus = 'Y'.
