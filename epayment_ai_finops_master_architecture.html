0.1. Operational Scale (1.8M+ TEU), Bills of Lading & Logistics Surcharges

  • Global Fleet Scale: Ocean Network Express (ONE) was established in 2017 through the joint merger of Japan's three legendary shipping lines (NYK Line, MOL, and "K" Line). The carrier operates a modern fleet of 250+ container vessels representing over 1.8 Million TEUs (Twenty-Foot Equivalent Units), connecting 120+ nations worldwide with its iconic Magenta branding.
  • The Bill of Lading (B/L) as a Triple Legal Instrument: In maritime law, a Bill of Lading is not merely a receipt. It is a 3-in-1 legal document serving as:
    1. A formal Receipt of Cargo loaded onboard the vessel.
    2. The binding Contract of Carriage between the carrier and shipper.
    3. A negotiable Document of Title—whoever holds the endorsed original or authorized digital equivalent possesses legal ownership of the physical cargo.
    The architecture must natively support both Master B/L (MBL) issued directly by the ocean carrier and House B/L (HBL) issued by intermediary freight forwarders.
  • The 6 Critical Freight Charges & Penalties:
    • OF (Ocean Freight): The base line-haul ocean transportation rate.
    • THC (Terminal Handling Charges): Crane, equipment, and stevedoring fees at the port terminal.
    • DEM (Demurrage): Penalties assessed when loaded containers remain inside the port container yard beyond agreed free days.
    • DET (Detention): Penalties incurred when the customer holds empty or unpacked containers outside the terminal past agreed free days.
    • EMF (Equipment Management Fee): Cleaning, maintenance, and administrative container handling fees.
    • LSS (Low Sulfur Surcharge): Environmental fuel regulation compliance surcharges (IMO 2020).

  • Partial Container Independent Release: If a single container in a 10-container B/L is flagged for customs inspection, the remaining 9 containers are unblocked and issued valid e-D/O release tokens immediately, preventing logistics gridlock.

import { createHmac, timingSafeEqual } from 'crypto'

/**
 * Generates a URL-safe Base64 HMAC-SHA256 signature
 */
export function createHash(input: string, secret: string): string {
  const hmac = createHmac('sha256', secret)
  hmac.update(input)
  return hmac.digest('base64url') // Eliminates '+', '/', '=' to ensure clean URL query parsing
}

/**
 * Validates signature with constant-time comparison to prevent side-channel timing attacks
 */
export function verifyHash(input: string, hash: string, secret: string): boolean {
  const expected = createHash(input, secret)
  if (expected.length !== hash.length) return false
  return timingSafeEqual(Buffer.from(expected), Buffer.from(hash))
}
