01 Maritime Freight Logistics & Corporate FinTech Economics

💡 Business Reality: Why Maritime Invoicing is Nothing Like B2C E-Commerce

In ocean shipping, orders are not $50 retail shopping carts—an average B2B invoice is $50,000 to $100,000 USD across dozens of shipping containers. In the legacy workflow, freight forwarders paid via manual wire transfers, sent bank deposit slips over email, and waited 24 to 48 hours for human accountants to verify and release terminal holds in SAP. Meanwhile, trucks sat stranded outside port gates, and forwarders incurred $2,000 to $10,000 per day in port demurrage penalties at midnight. Our platform was engineered to collapse this entire clearing lifecycle into under 60 seconds.

1.2. The 3-Tier Safety Net: Zero Dropped Webhooks

In high-value B2B payment systems, dropping a webhook notification means customer money is debited but the container remains stuck at the port terminal. We guarantee 100% reconciliation through a 3-tier safety net:

  • Tier 1: Event-Driven Fast Path (99% of transactions): Webhook notifications from HSBC Omni, VietQR, or PromptPay arrive in real time, processed by our Go broker in 4.2ms.
  • Tier 2: Active Recovery Poller (Every 5 minutes): If a webhook drops due to transient network failure, a background poller queries the bank inquiry API, or the user clicks "Check Payment" to self-heal in 500ms.
  • Tier 3: Midnight SFTP Reconciliation (23:59:00 Daily): An automated cron worker pulls the official MT940 / CAMT.053 electronic bank statement via encrypted SFTP, sweeping all bank transit items and resolving any unposted credits with 100% accuracy.

02 Data Architecture: Why the Operational Core Needs Only 2 Collections

🏛️ SAP S/4HANA as the Single Source of Truth (SSOT)

In standard e-commerce architectures, databases sprawl across dozens of tables (Users, Invoices, Ledgers, Inventory, Wallets). In ONE's platform, the operational database consists of only 2 core collections: purchase_orders and payment_transactions. SAP S/4HANA holds 100% of accounting and maritime master data; duplicating tables in MongoDB would cause catastrophic data drift.

🎙️ HOW TO EXPLAIN THE 2-COLLECTION DESIGN (SPOKEN SCRIPT)
"Because SAP S/4HANA is our Single Source of Truth for all accounting and maritime master data, we strictly avoid duplicating customer tables, general ledgers, or container inventories—doing so would cause catastrophic data drift. At our smart edge layer, we only track two business concepts:
Purchase Order (PO) answers 'What are we paying for?' (the invoice debt snapshot).
Payment Transaction answers 'How are we paying?' (the banking attempts and session states).
This clean Document Aggregate Root pattern delivers sub-millisecond writes and eliminates multi-table joins completely."
💡 Ý chính dễ nhớ: SAP S/4HANA là nguồn chân lý duy nhất. Không nhân bản bảng khách hay cước vào Mongo để tránh lệch data. Mongo chỉ cần 2 collection: PO (nợ hóa đơn nào) và Transaction (các lần thử thanh toán).

2.1. Why Distributed 2PC is Fatal Across Enterprise Boundaries

Two-Phase Commit (2PC) requires holding distributed database locks and SAP table locks across external network roundtrips. In ocean shipping where SAP ERPs handle scheduled maintenance, 2PC causes distributed deadlocks and freezes the entire payment portal. We implement the Saga Pattern via Temporal: each local step commits atomically. If SAP fails or rejects an order downstream, Temporal executes an automated Compensating Transaction—refunding the bank payment and restoring the invoice status deterministically.

03 Edge Ingestion: 4.2ms Go Webhook Broker & Distributed Mutex

⚡ Concurrency Bottleneck: CPU-Bound RSA Signature Verification

When partner banks flush tens of thousands of queued webhooks after weekend maintenance (5,000 RPS burst), Node.js event loops freeze under CPU-intensive RSA-SHA256 signature verification. In Go, lightweight goroutines verify cryptographic signatures across CPU cores in parallel. The broker locks a Redis idempotency key in 0.3ms, buffers the event to Temporal, and returns 200 OK to the bank in exactly 4.2ms P99, sustaining 5,000 RPS on just 45MB of RAM per pod.

🎙️ HOW TO EXPLAIN THE 4.2MS GO BROKER (SPOKEN SCRIPT)
"When our partner banks flush tens of thousands of queued webhooks after maintenance, Node.js event loops freeze under CPU-heavy RSA signature verification. In Go, lightweight goroutines verify RSA-SHA256 signatures in parallel across CPU cores in under 1ms. The broker locks a Redis idempotency key in 0.3ms, triggers a singleton Temporal workflow, and returns 200 OK to the bank in exactly 4.2ms P99. It sustains 5,000 RPS on only 45MB of RAM per pod, keeping our front door wide open."
💡 Ý chính dễ nhớ: Ngân hàng xả webhook dồn dập sau bảo trì, Node.js bị nghẽn Event Loop do giải mã RSA tốn CPU. Go dùng Goroutines chia đều ra đa nhân, trả 200 OK trong 4.2ms và tốn chỉ 45MB RAM.

3.1. Preventing Multi-Tab Double Payments: Redis MSETNX & Storage Backstop

Freight forwarders frequently open 5 browser tabs and click "Pay" simultaneously right before midnight to beat the demurrage clock. We enforce strict idempotency through a two-layer defense:

  • Layer 1 (0.3ms In-Memory Mutex): The Go broker executes an atomic Redis MSETNX command across all constituent invoice keys in the batch (lock:invoice:INV_01, lock:invoice:INV_02, TTL 900s). Only the first thread succeeds; concurrent tabs fail immediately with HTTP 409 Conflict.
  • Layer 2 (Database Unique Constraint): A MongoDB compound unique index on purchase_order_id + transaction_reference acts as a permanent storage backstop, guaranteeing exactly-once financial clearing even during complete Redis failover.

04 AI FinOps Pipeline: 258ms Multimodal Tax Extraction & Vector Mapping

🧠 The Asian Tax Certificate Challenge & Zero Blind Trust

Across Asian logistics hubs (Thailand WHT 1%, Vietnam PIT, China Fapiao), freight forwarders are legally entitled to withhold local taxes before remitting freight payment. In legacy workflows, forwarders uploaded low-quality scans of paper tax withholding certificates, requiring human accountants to spend 2 to 3 days verifying tax IDs, seal stamps, and line items. Containers sat stranded at port gates during this verification delay.

🎙️ HOW TO EXPLAIN THE AI TAX PIPELINE (SPOKEN SCRIPT)
"We engineered a 4-step pipeline: First, C++ PyMuPDF and OpenCV deskew and sharpen the scanned PDF in 18ms, compressing it to 280KB—which fits into exactly 4 Gemini vision tiles. Second, we cache our entire 3,200-token system prompt and tax schema on Vertex AI RAM using Context Caching, cutting token costs by 77% and latency to 258ms. Third, we embed the extracted freight descriptions into 256-dimensional vectors and run HNSW semantic search on Redis in 1.2ms to match standard SAP condition codes. Finally, we run a math verification in SAP. The result is 98.4% zero-touch automated clearing."
💡 Ý chính dễ nhớ: C++ OpenCV nén ảnh trong 18ms, Vertex AI RAM cache prompt giảm 77% tiền token, Gemini Flash trả JSON trong 258ms, và Redis Vector DB map mã cước SAP trong 1.2ms.

4.1. The 4-Step Production AI Architecture

  1. Native C++ OpenCV & PyMuPDF Preprocessing (18ms): Automatically deskews rotations, enhances contrast with CLAHE, and removes scanner noise. Compresses 15MB scanned PDFs down to 280KB, strictly bound to 4 Gemini vision tiles.
  2. Vertex AI RAM Context Caching (258ms): The static 3,200-token prompt—containing complex tax withholding laws, JSON schemas, and extraction rules—is cached in Vertex AI RAM. Incoming requests send only the 4 image tiles, slashing API costs by 77%.
  3. Redis Vector DB HNSW Entity Resolution (1.2ms): Shippers submit thousands of localized, typo-ridden descriptions for the same charge (e.g., 'Phí vệ sinh container', 'Washing fee', 'Cleaning surcharge'). An embedding model generates 256-dimensional vectors, and Redis HNSW cosine similarity search maps them deterministically to standard SAP condition codes.
  4. Zero Blind Trust Governance (SOX 404 Compliance): To satisfy Big-4 financial audit standards, an automated sampler routes 5% of all AI-cleared transactions to a human accountant side-by-side reconciliation queue, while any extraction with confidence <90% diverts to human review before clearing.

05 Distributed Saga Orchestration: 14-Country Engine & SAP S/4HANA Clearing

The platform coordinates payments across 14 countries, each with unique banking protocols, local tax laws, and ERP posting rules. We implement the Smart Dispatcher Pattern via Temporal.io: the API gateway remains completely stateless, normalizing incoming webhooks and dispatching them to country-specific Child Workflows (vn-hsbc-omni-to-sap, th-ai-wht-document-analysis, cn-boc-b2b-to-sap). Updating banking protocols in Vietnam has zero blast radius on Thailand or China.

5.1. Dual SAP Clearing Streams: Freight Receivables vs. Container Deposits

When funds arrive at ONE's corporate bank accounts, Temporal orchestrates two parallel financial streams:

  • General Ocean Freight Clearing (SAP FI-AR): Calls BAPI_ACC_DOCUMENT_POST to clear open customer receivables and issue an official financial voucher.
  • Container Security Deposit Management (SAP BRIM FICA): Security deposits ($500–$2,000/container) are posted to SAP BRIM, issuing a unique deposit voucher (DEPOSITREQNUM) tied to the container seal. When truckers return empty, undamaged containers to the depot, an Equipment Interchange Receipt (EIR) is recorded, triggering an automated bank refund without human accounting touches.
  • Instant Port Cargo Release (< 60 Seconds): As soon as SAP confirms clearing, Temporal dispatches an encrypted electronic Delivery Order (e-D/O) directly to the Port Terminal Operating System (TOS), lifting the container hold in under 60 seconds.

5.2. Automated Saga Compensating Reversals

If an irrecoverable exception occurs after customer funds have settled into ONE's bank account—such as an unresolvable tax discrepancy or a downstream ERP lock—Temporal triggers a 5-step Saga Compensating Refund:

  1. Verifies the transaction state and locks the record.
  2. Calls the bank refund API (HSBC Omni or PromptPay) to return 100% of funds to the customer account.
  3. Invokes SAP official reversal BAPI (BAPI_ACC_DOCUMENT_REV_POST), generating a reversal voucher that reopens the invoice debt and zeroes the bank transit ledger.
  4. Releases all Redis mutex locks so the forwarder can re-attempt checkout.
  5. Emits an immutable audit event for financial reconciliation.

06 Edge Infrastructure: Apache APISIX on GCP & Zero-Worker-Reload

🌐 Why Apache APISIX Over NGINX Ingress or Kong

Under high-frequency Kubernetes pod autoscaling, traditional NGINX Ingress reloads its master and worker processes to update upstream routing tables, causing packet drops and severed WebSockets during traffic spikes. Apache APISIX uses etcd dynamic watchers to sync routing rules in shared memory with zero worker reloads. On top of that, APISIX validates public RSA keys (JWKS) directly in worker RAM, completing token authentication in 0.02ms (52,000 RPS per pod).

6.1. Micro-Frontend Architecture with Webpack Module Federation

Our portal serves 14 countries with unique regional workflows: Thai users need withholding tax upload forms; Taiwanese users require E-GUI invoice selector modals; Vietnamese users need VietQR canvas rendering. With Webpack Module Federation, each regional team develops, tests, and deploys their remote micro-frontend independently. The host container shell consumes these remotes at runtime. Regional deployments take 2 minutes instead of waiting for a 45-minute monolithic build.

07 Database Concurrency: Solving WiredTiger 128-Ticket Contention

🔥 Production Incident: 94% CPU at Only 420 TPS

During a month-end financial closing, our MongoDB Atlas CPU spiked to 94%, and query latency jumped from 5ms to over 4,200ms. Shipping container releases were at risk of being blocked worldwide. Hardware was not the bottleneck—actual write throughput was only 420 ops/sec.

🎙️ HOW TO EXPLAIN WIREDTIGER TICKET CONTENTION (SPOKEN SCRIPT)
"It was a classic connection pool misconfiguration. 30 GKE pods with default `maxPoolSize=100` established 3,000 active sockets fighting for WiredTiger's 128 internal read tickets. Available tickets dropped to zero, and the Linux kernel wasted all CPU cycles on thread context switching. By scientifically bounding connection pools to `maxPoolSize=15` per pod (450 total sockets) and offloading read-heavy dashboard queries to secondary replicas, available tickets stayed consistently above 70, query latency dropped from 4,200ms to 6ms, and CPU cooled down to 32%."
💡 Ý chính dễ nhớ: 30 pod Mongo x 100 connection = 3,000 socket tranh nhau 128 vé đọc WiredTiger, CPU chết vì context switching. Giới hạn maxPoolSize=15 mỗi pod, CPU hạ từ 95% xuống 32%, latency giảm từ 4.2s còn 6ms.

7.1. Keyset Cursor Pagination on 25.4M Records: 1.62ms O(1) flat

Traditional offset pagination (skip(50000).limit(20)) is an $O(N)$ linear scan: the database must traverse 50,000 B-tree index entries and discard them before reading 20 rows. In our audit log of 25.4 million records, page 5,000 took 5.4 seconds. We replaced it with Keyset Cursor Pagination using composite index bounds: 

// O(1) B-Tree Direct Seek: returns in 1.62ms flat regardless of page depth
db.audit_logs.find({
  $or: [
    { createdAt: { $lt: cursorDate } },
    { createdAt: cursorDate, _id: { $lt: cursorId } }
  ]
}).sort({ createdAt: -1, _id: -1 }).limit(20);

08 Security Architecture, Zero-Trust & ArgoCD GitOps

8.1. Hybrid Identity: Okta Passport vs. Internal Port Security Badge

An Okta token is like a National Passport: it proves identity (who you are), but its enterprise tokens are bloated with Active Directory groups (exceeding 4KB), causing HTTP 431 header errors across microservices. At our edge APISIX gateway, we exchange it for a lightweight 400-byte internal token that injects exact functional scopes (e.g., can_release_container:SGN_PORT, currency approval thresholds). This eliminates header bloat and prevents vendor lock-in.

8.2. Double HttpOnly Cookies with __Host- Prefix

We store tokens in Double HttpOnly cookies with the __Host- prefix and SameSite=Strict. Because they are HttpOnly, JavaScript cannot read them—making them 100% immune to XSS theft. Because they are marked SameSite=Strict and require a custom anti-CSRF request header (X-Requested-With), cross-origin requests are blocked by the browser. The access token lives for 15 minutes, and the refresh token rotates on every use with a 15-second grace period for safe tab recovery.

8.3. GitOps Progressive Delivery with ArgoCD & Automated Rollback

  • Wave 0 (PreSync Hook): A Kubernetes Job executes database migrations using the Expand-Contract pattern before new pods deploy. If the migration fails, deployment halts immediately.
  • Wave 1 (Canary 10% Traffic): New pods receive 10% of traffic. Argo Rollouts monitors Prometheus metrics for 10 minutes.
  • 2-Second Automated Rollback: If HTTP 5xx errors exceed 0.1% or p95 latency crosses 800ms, traffic automatically rolls back 100% in under 2 seconds without human intervention.

09 Production SRE: Bulkhead Isolation, Circuit Breakers & Observability

9.1. The Maritime Bulkhead Pattern for Connection Pools

Just as a container ship partitions its hull into watertight compartments to prevent sinking, we split our MongoDB connection pool into isolated compartments:

  • 35 dedicated sockets for live port checkouts and bank webhooks.
  • 10 dedicated sockets for heavy month-end finance Excel reports.
  • 5 dedicated sockets for background queue workers.

Even if finance runs a 10-million-row tax audit query, it only saturates its 10 slots—live QR payments at port gates never experience connection starvation.

9.2. Granular Circuit Breakers with Passive Canary Probing
